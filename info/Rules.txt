# Rules #

1. Try to keep all conversations civil. We don't want arguments. It saves us all a headache.

2. No extremely sexual or suggestive messages. Keep that in DMs. It can make a lot of people uncomfortable. This includes but not limited to: Images, Text, Websites, Voice Messages, etc.

> Extremely sexual messages are things that contain kinks/bdsm, sex lives, sexual acts, ect.

> Suggestive messages are as they sound, stuff that isn't extremely sexual but hints at sexual acts with or towards another member (including yourself).

> This is includes references to adult films/content in memes.

3. Please take sensitive topics seriously. Even if you don't care about what's happening, show some respect for others.

4. Don't try to bypass any filters put in place. If you find any bypasses or errors with filtering, alert moderators via DMs.

> including images, gifs, vc, ect!

5. If you have a friend that wants to join Larp Nation, **please** ask one of the owners before you just invite them.

6. Respect others

> i.e. no slurs, insults, hate speech, ect...

7. Any other rules that isn't explicitly mentioned here that you see in other Discord servers must also be followed. On that note, follow Discord TOS.

8. Don't try to find a loophole with these rules.

9. Swearing is allowed however use of slurs, highly offensive/deragotory language is not permitted in Larpnation.

10. Follow the same rules as you would here outside of this server. For example, Don't go harassing members via DMs.

11. If you don't know what's going on in a situation, please don't say anything at all until you know the full story. I.E. read the room!

> This is to avoid more added drama in the server! We'd like to stay drama free as much as possible.

12. All of these rules must be followed in all channels of this server, including voice-chats!

13. It's not up to others to tell if you're feeling uncomfortable. If you're feeling uncomfortable, explicitly tell us.

> Use the #create-a-ticket channel!

How Warnings or Timeouts happen.
There are multiple ways to get warnings or a timeout, this includes:

> Going against the rules despite being verbally warned to stop. Attempting to find loopholes within the rules. Making other members uncomfortable/crossing boundaries and refusing to change your actions! Abusing Tickets!

**This is a three warnings to one strike system,** meaning if you hit three warnings then you will be provided a strike from the server and would have to submit an appeal to be unbanned!

> aka. one strike = 3 warnings, and 9 warnings = a ban from the server!

**Warnings are appealable,** you simply have to create a ticket and explain why you believe you've been falsely warned, or why you think your warning should be appealed! **We reserve all rights to deny you an appeal if we deem so!**

**Timeouts can vary in time,** depending on your actions you may get timed out for a day, a few hours, or maybe a few minutes depending on the offense you commited.
